Summit on Global Governance Proposes Unified Digital Identity Protocol

By Rachel Thorne | Published July 30, 2026

BERLIN — Representatives from 35 nations drafted a preliminary charter establishing cross-border interoperability for sovereign digital identities.

Designed to safeguard personal privacy while streamlining international travel, legal documentation, and banking, the system relies on decentralized user-controlled credentials.

Participating governments emphasized that individuals will retain full ownership and control over their personal data attributes.
