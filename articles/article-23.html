Global Commercial Real Estate Sector Adapts to Distributed Workforce Models

By Marcus Vance | Published July 30, 2026

LONDON — Commercial property developers in major financial hubs are rapidly repurposing traditional office towers into hybrid co-working hubs, residential units, and urban indoor vertical farms.

The architectural shift comes as global corporations permanently adopt flexible hybrid work models, prioritizing eco-certified flexible space over static long-term leases.

Real estate investment trusts (REITs) specializing in adaptive reuse properties outperformed conventional commercial portfolios by 14% over the past year.
