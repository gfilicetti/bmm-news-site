Bipartisan Coalition Introduces Landmark Autonomous AI Ethics Bill

By Rachel Thorne | Published July 30, 2026
▶ BMM VIDEO REPORT

BMM Capitol Coverage: Congressional sponsors discuss algorithmic auditing and safety overrides in newly introduced legislation.

WASHINGTON — A bipartisan coalition of lawmakers unveiled comprehensive legislation today governing the deployment of autonomous decision-making algorithms in critical public sectors.

The bill mandates algorithmic auditing, mandatory human-in-the-loop overrides for safety-critical infrastructure, and strict transparency requirements for automated decision engines.

Industry advocacy groups and consumer rights organization voiced cautious support for the balanced regulatory framework.
