Deep-Space Array Detects Repeating Signals from Outer Rim

By David Sterling | Published July 30, 2026
▶ BMM VIDEO REPORT

BMM Science Report: Radio astronomers detail periodic signals originating 1.2 billion light-years away.

SOCORRO, NEW MEXICO — Astrophysicists monitoring the Next-Generation Very Large Array (ngVLA) recorded a series of ultra-short, highly periodic radio bursts originating 1.2 billion light-years away in a distant starburst galaxy.

Unlike standard Fast Radio Bursts (FRBs), the signals exhibit a precise 16.3-minute repeating pattern across three distinct harmonic frequency bands.

Teams across international observatories are aligning optical and X-ray instruments to pinpoint the progenitor system and determine whether magnetar activity or extreme orbital physics are responsible.
