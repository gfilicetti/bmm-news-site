Orbital Satellite Mesh Guarantees Gigabit Broadband Worldwide

By Kenneth Takahashi | Published July 30, 2026

SINGAPORE — Telecom regulators confirmed the successful deployment and inter-satellite laser linking of the final constellation tier of the GlobalMesh orbital network.

With 4,200 active low-Earth orbit (LEO) nodes connected via optical cross-links, high-speed gigabit throughput is now accessible in previously remote maritime, polar, and interior desert regions with sub-15 millisecond latency.

The infrastructure project opens new avenues for remote telemetry, emergency response, and localized offshore edge computing.
