James Webb Successor Telescope Captures Atmosphere of Exoplanet LHS 1140b

By Dr. Maya Lin | Published July 30, 2026

PASADENA — Astrophysics teams analyzing transmission spectroscopy data from orbital observatories confirmed the detection of water vapor, nitrogen, and carbon dioxide in the atmosphere of temperate super-Earth LHS 1140b.

Located 48 light-years away in the constellation Cetus, the planet orbits within its host star's habitable zone and may harbor a global liquid ocean beneath its cloud cover.

Astronomers describe the atmospheric signature as the most compelling candidate yet for habitable conditions outside our solar system.
