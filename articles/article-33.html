Breakthrough Synthetic Protein Reverses Cellular Aging Markers in Lab Tests

By Dr. Aris Thorne | Published July 30, 2026

CAMBRIDGE — Molecular biologists at Harvard and Oxford unveiled a engineered protein complex capable of selectively clearing senescent cells and restoring mitochondrial efficiency in tissue cultures.

In preclinical mouse models, the treatment extended median lifespan by 28% while improving muscle density, cognitive function, and cardiovascular health.

Researchers are preparing Phase 1 clinical trials targeting age-related neurodegenerative conditions.
