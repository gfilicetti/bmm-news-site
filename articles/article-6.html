Atlantic Drone Fleet Maps Deep-Sea Thermal Currents in Unprecedented Detail

By Liam O'Connor | Published July 30, 2026

REYKJAVIK — An autonomous fleet of 120 deep-diving ocean Gliders completed a 90-day survey of the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (AMOC), transmitting high-density thermal and salinity profiles back to land stations.

The mission revealed previously unmapped deep-water thermal eddies that assist in modulating northern hemisphere weather patterns during seasonal transitions.

Oceanographers state the dataset provides critical baseline physics for calibrating global atmospheric warming models for the coming half-century.
