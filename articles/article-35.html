Opinion: Why Decentralized Power Grids Are Essential for Climate Resilience

By Elena Rostova | Published July 30, 2026

Centralized electricity distribution architectures built in the 20th century were designed for predictable fossil-fuel inputs and centralized generation plants. Today, they represent vulnerable single points of failure.

Transitioning to localized microgrids powered by rooftop solar, community energy storage, and smart peer-to-peer trading algorithms ensures communities remain powered during extreme weather events.

Decentralized power is not merely an environmental goal; it is a fundamental pillar of national security and civic infrastructure.
