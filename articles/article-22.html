Venture Capital Inflows Hit Record High for Next-Gen Clean Energy Startups

By Elena Rostova | Published July 30, 2026

SAN FRANCISCO — Global venture capital investments in climate technology startups surpassed $85 billion in the first half of 2026, setting a new historical benchmark.

Funding was concentrated in next-generation battery chemistries, direct air carbon capture technology, and green hydrogen electrolysis.

Investment directors attribute the capital boom to strong regulatory incentives and growing commercial demand for decarbonized industrial supply chains.
