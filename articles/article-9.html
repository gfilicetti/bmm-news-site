VR Cinema Wins Top Honors at International Film Festival

By Chloe Bennett | Published July 30, 2026

VENICE — The jury of the 83rd Venice International Film Festival awarded the coveted Golden Lion for Best Picture to an immersive spatial narrative film designed exclusively for neural VR headsets.

Titled Echoes of the Horizon, the film allows viewers to dynamically navigate alternate character viewpoints in real time without breaking dramatic cohesion.

Critics hailed the project as a watershed moment where spatial computing and traditional cinematic storytelling officially merged.
