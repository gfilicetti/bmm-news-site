Urban Mobility Initiative Expands Autonomous Bus Fleet

By Elena Rostova | Published July 30, 2026

SINGAPORE — Municipal transit authorities today announced the second phase deployment of 400 fully autonomous, zero-emission electric buses across key metropolitan transit corridors.

Equipped with solid-state LiDAR sensors and real-time V2X (Vehicle-to-Everything) communication arrays, the fleet has logged over two million passenger kilometers with zero safety incidents.

City planners project the automated transit expansion will reduce peak-hour traffic congestion by 22% while cutting municipal transit operating costs.
