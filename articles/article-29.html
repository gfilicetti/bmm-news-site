AI Diagnostics Tool Detects Early-Stage Oncology Markers with 98% Accuracy

By Dr. Aris Thorne | Published July 30, 2026

LONDON — A international multi-center study published in *The Lancet* demonstrated that a machine learning diagnostic algorithm successfully identified early-stage cancer markers from standard blood liquid biopsies years before physical symptoms appear.

Analyzing circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) fragments across 50,000 patient samples, the AI platform achieved a 98.4% sensitivity rate with negligible false positives.

Oncologists emphasize that early detection via routine annual blood screening could dramatically improve long-term survival outcomes.
