Subatomic Particle Experiment Discovers Previously Unobserved Boson Coupling

By Dr. Maya Lin | Published July 30, 2026

GENEVA — Physicists at the High-Luminosity Large Hadron Collider announced the statistical confirmation of a rare decay channel involving Higgs bosons and heavy flavor quarks.

Achieving a 5.2-sigma significance threshold, the observation matches high-precision predictions of the Standard Model while imposing tighter constraints on theoretical supersymmetry models.

Researchers plan to utilize the high-luminosity run data to probe dark matter candidates over the next decade.
