Opinion: Reclaiming Human Creativity in the Era of Generative Synthetic Media

By Clara Dupont | Published July 30, 2026

In a world where hyper-realistic synthetic imagery, music, and text can be generated in milliseconds, what becomes of genuine human artistic expression?

Far from rendering human artists obsolete, the proliferation of AI media is creating a profound cultural appreciation for authentic human craftsmanship, live performance, and raw emotional storytelling.

True art has never been solely about the final output; it is defined by the human experience, vulnerability, and intentionality behind every creation.
