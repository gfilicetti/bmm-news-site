Global Energy Summit Reaches Historic Agreement on Next-Gen Fusion Grid Standards

By Sarah Jenkins | Published July 30, 2026

VIENNA — Energy ministers from 42 nations signed the Vienna Fusion Accord today, establishing unified safety and distribution protocols for commercially operated magnet-confined fusion reactors expected on the grid by 2029.
