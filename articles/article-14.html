Global Infrastructure Summit Focuses on Resilient Bridges

By David Sterling | Published July 30, 2026

BRUSSELS — Delegates from over fifty nations convened in Brussels today for the 2026 Infrastructure Resilience Summit, agreeing to a $60 billion global fund dedicated to upgrading aging transportation corridors.

The initiative prioritizes structural sensor retrofits, climate-adaptive concrete formulations, and high-load suspension designs along critical trade arteries.

Ministers emphasized that resilient transport networks are vital to preventing supply chain bottlenecks caused by extreme weather events.
