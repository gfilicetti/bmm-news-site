Global Health Organization Declares Elimination of Trachoma in 12 Nations

By Dr. Aris Thorne | Published July 30, 2026

GENEVA — Health officials celebrated a major milestone in global disease eradication as twelve nations across Africa and South Asia officially eliminated blinding trachoma as a public health problem.

The achievement was made possible by sustained community-based antibiotic distribution, water sanitation improvements, and facial hygiene education initiatives.

Public health leaders credited international health partnerships and dedicated community healthcare workers for eradicating the ancient neglected tropical disease.
