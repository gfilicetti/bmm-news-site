Universal mRNA Vaccine Platform Enters Phase 3 Clinical Trials

By Dr. Priya Nair | Published July 30, 2026

BOSTON — The World Health Organization and global biotech partners announced the start of Phase 3 human trials for a pan-lineage mRNA platform engineered to protect against rapidly mutating respiratory viral strains.

Unlike seasonal boosters that target specific genetic variants, the universal platform targets highly conserved structural proteins that remain invariant across viral mutations.

Over 45,000 participants across six continents have enrolled in the double-blind study, with initial efficacy readouts expected by late November.
