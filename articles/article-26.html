Next-Generation Solid-State Batteries Enter Mass Production for Electronics

By Elena Rostova | Published July 30, 2026

OSAKA — Battery manufacturing executives confirmed that the first commercial production lines for solid-state lithium-metal batteries have commenced full-scale operation.

Offering double the energy density of conventional lithium-ion cells alongside zero risk of thermal runaway, the batteries promise to significantly extend smartphone battery life and electric vehicle ranges.

Initial shipments are scheduled to reach mobile device manufacturers by the end of Q4 2026.
