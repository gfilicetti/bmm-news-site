Central Banks Announce Unified Protocol for Digital Settlements

By Elena Rostova | Published July 30, 2026

NEW YORK — In a landmark joint session, representatives from sixteen major central banks released the finalized standard for cross-border Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) interbank clearing.

The new framework, named the Global Ledger Interoperability Standard (GLIS), aims to reduce settlement times for international commercial transfers from three business days to less than two seconds, while eliminating intermediate conversion fees.

Financial analysts predict the move could unlock over $140 billion annually in trapped liquidity across international trade corridors. Rollout of the pilot settlement nodes is scheduled to begin in Q1 2027.
