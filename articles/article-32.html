Neurotech Implant Restores Mobility for Paralysis Patients in Clinical Trial

By Sarah Jenkins | Published July 30, 2026

LAUSANNE — Medical researchers in Switzerland reported that nine individuals with complete spinal cord injuries regained voluntary walking ability following the implantation of an epidural electrical stimulation array.

The wireless system creates a digital bridge between the brain and the lower spinal cord, decoding movement intent in real time and delivering precise electrical pulses to leg muscles.

All participants in the study were able to stand, walk with assistance, and climb stairs after a few months of neuro-rehabilitation.
