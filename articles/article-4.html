Lunar Base 'Artemis IV' Unveils First Bio-Dome Agricultural Harvest

By Marcus Vance | Published July 30, 2026

SHACKLETON CRATER, MOON — Astronauts stationed at the Artemis IV lunar research outpost harvested their first yield of hydroponically grown crops inside the newly pressurized Bio-Dome Beta early Thursday morning.

The yield included fast-growth varieties of dwarf wheat, leafy greens, and high-protein legumes, all cultivated using recycled lunar condensate and treated regolith nutrients.

“Demonstrating self-sustaining food production in low gravity is a vital prerequisite for long-duration Martian transit,” stated Mission Commander Dr. Aris Thorne during a live broadcast from the lunar surface.
